# Contributing content

## Writing

- Write for one audience per page, in plain language. Lead with what the reader should do.
- Short sentences and paragraphs; use tables for comparisons and numbered lists for steps.
- Put emergency actions first where they apply, in a red `danger` admonition.
- Colour is never the only signal: condition levels always carry a text label (Low / Moderate / High).
- The portal is organisation-agnostic: no agency logos, taglines or brand artwork. Sources are cited by title and link.

## Sources

- Base content on official Singapore standards, legislation, approved codes of practice, official health and emergency guidance, and peer-reviewed or international consensus sources.
- **Never copy standards verbatim.** Paraphrase, organise by audience and cite the clause, e.g. "Based on SS 681:2022, clause 9". Rebuild tables, forms and checklists in the portal's own design.
- Quote legislation only briefly and link to the official statute.
- Mark guidance that goes beyond a source as portal good practice.
- Every content page ends with a **Based on** list and a **Last reviewed** date.

## Risk assessment

All risk content uses the 5x5 matrix from the Code of Practice on WSH Risk Management, with portal bands Low 1–3, Medium 4–12, High 15–25.

## Review

- Medical content cites consensus statements, peer-reviewed journals and official health guidance.
- Review each page at least yearly, and whenever a cited source changes.
