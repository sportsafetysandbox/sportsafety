# Play safe. Plan safe. Stay safe.

Practical guidance, standards and free tools to manage safety in sport in Singapore, for anyone who takes part in, coaches, organises, hosts or governs sport.
</div>

!!! danger "Someone collapsed, not breathing or badly hurt?"
    **Call 995 now**, start CPR and get an AED. [What to do in an emergency](emergency/when-to-call-995.md)

    Build a safety management system and lead safety as a board or executive team.
