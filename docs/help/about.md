# About the Portal

The Sport Safety Portal brings together guidance, standards and practical tools to help everyone involved in sport in Singapore manage safety: participants and the public, coaches and exercise professionals, activity organisers, facility operators and sport organisations.

## How the portal is organised

- **I am a...** pathways tell each audience what they need to do.
- **Safety Topics** hold one authoritative page per hazard (heat, concussion, drowning and so on). Pathway pages link to them rather than repeating them.
- **Toolkit** holds templates, checklists and tools to download and adapt.
- **Standards & Guidelines** lists the standards, codes and laws behind the guidance.

## How content is written

- Content is written in plain language from **official Singapore standards and legislation**, **approved codes of practice**, **official health and emergency guidance**, and **peer-reviewed or international consensus sources**.
- Every content page lists what it is **Based on** and shows a **Last reviewed** date.
- Standards are paraphrased and cited by clause, never reproduced. To apply a standard in full, obtain it from its publisher.
- Where guidance goes beyond a cited source, it is marked as portal good practice.
- Medical content is based on published consensus statements, peer-reviewed journals and official health guidance. It is not a substitute for advice from a doctor.

## Risk assessment method

All risk assessment guidance and templates on this portal use the 5x5 risk matrix from the Code of Practice on WSH Risk Management, so that sport risk assessments line up with statutory workplace risk management.

## Status

The portal is in development. Pages marked **Page in development** show planned content only. Pages marked **Pilot page** are drafted for review.
